# Terminal component notices

The browser terminal uses [xterm.js](https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js), including its fit addon, under the MIT license. Browser assets are bundled locally, with additional upstream notices next to the generated JavaScript/CSS.

The CRT appearance is an independently implemented browser theme inspired by [cool-retro-term](https://github.com/Swordfish90/cool-retro-term). The Qt application and its source are not bundled.
